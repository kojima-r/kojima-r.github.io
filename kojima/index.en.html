I am studying artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning technology to apply them to real-world problems. My interests include the integration of deep learning and modeling technologies such as time-series modeling, probabilistic modeling, and logic programming. Using these technologies, I am also working on real-world applications: medical applications, drug discovery/chemoinformatics, bioinformatics, ecology (bird song analysis), and robotics (robot audition).
